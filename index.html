Import data

Export these lists from MN Program to Excel or CSV and select them together. The files are read in this browser and are not uploaded.

  • Appointments: patient number, date, status, professional, room, type
  • Treatments: patient number, treatment, amount, status, date accepted, date done, room
  • Payments: patient number, payment date, amount, payment method
  • Patients (optional): patient number, registration date

Patient names are not needed. Leave them out of the export if you can.
